The Fine Print

Privacy

Plain English. No tracking, no selling, no surprises.

What we collect

When you RSVP we store your name, email, and the answers you give on the form (graduation year, location, attendance status, opt-ins, and anything you write in the comments box). Phone number and social handles are optional; you only share them if you fill them in.

How we use it

  • To plan the reunion (headcount, dietary notes, accessibility needs).
  • To email you reunion updates — only if you opted in.
  • The "Who's Going" list shows your first name, last initial, city/state, and any social handles you chose to share. Email and phone are never shown publicly.
  • If you upload a photo to the Gallery, the email you submitted is verified against your RSVP and the photo is reviewed before it's published. Your email is never shown alongside the photo.

Where it lives

Data is stored in Supabase (Postgres). Photos are stored in Supabase Storage. Email is sent via Resend. We don't sell, share, or rent your information to anyone.

How long we keep it

We delete the entire database within 60 days after the reunion ends. If you want your record removed sooner, just email us.

Unsubscribing

Every email we send has an unsubscribe option. You can also reply with "unsubscribe" in the subject line. Unsubscribing removes you from email updates but keeps your RSVP on the headcount.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests: privacy contact.
